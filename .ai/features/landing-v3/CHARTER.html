4. Every later section: fewer words

Pricing, your data and the two ways in keep their facts and lose their sentences. Nothing claims more than the app does today.

Small calls Qareen takes, and the check
  • The job board reads 12 hiring tools today, so its line says 12, not 50.
  • The five demo tabs: Directory, Find a path, Ask for an intro, Asks board, Admin home, using the screenshots already on the page.
  • The rooms list keeps its ten rooms and the honest tags: Perks and Chat as Partly (half built), WhatsApp, Telegram and Signal as Coming (not built).
  • Pricing: two cards, the price and three short lines. Your data: six two-word facts. Two ways in: the form and the install line stay, the explaining sentences go.
  • Check: the page at phone, tablet and desktop width, light and dark, with and without motion, and the form still sends. A separate checker who did not build it reads every screen. About 2 hours to build, under 1 hour to check.
  • Undo: revert the pull request.
