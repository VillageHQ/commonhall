# Hero door field

What it does now (since 2026-11-15). Behind the landing page's first screen, a faint grid of doors drawn as on a floor plan
(72 px apart) waits at 6.5% ink on light, 9% on dark. Moving the pointer or a finger starts a circular wave; each door the
wave passes swings open 90 degrees with its swing arc, holds under a second, and shuts. Doors under the headline, the
buttons, the GitHub card and the blueprint house never open. Reduced motion: drawn once, closed. No JavaScript: not shown.
Chosen by the owner from four prototypes (plan-view arcs, front-view dominoes, ink ripples, chained dominoes).
