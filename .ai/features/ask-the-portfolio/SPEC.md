# Spec: "Ask the portfolio": an AI that answers from the portfolio's shared playbooks

**Added:** 2026-11-19 (round 5, wave 2)

## What & why
Know-how sits in shared playbooks and past answers, but finding it is slow.

## Rough scope
An assistant that searches the AI library, past asks and answers, and replies with links to the sources, then offers a warm intro to the author or a community ask when nothing fits. Needs an AI key (the fund's own, or Village's on the managed plan).

## Open questions
- Who pays for the AI key.
