## Findings (ranked by harm)

1. **The weekly email has no library or AI content.** Where: the weekly digest (`src/shell/weekly-email-compose.ts`, Sam's preview at `/admin/digest`). What they face: 14 links about intros, a profile line and their own asks, and 0 items about what other companies use. Why it stalls this persona: the email is how they come in, and it never shows the thing they care about, so they have no reason to open the library. They skim, see "intros waiting", and close it. **Harm 4.** Simpler default: add "New in the library: top 3 this week", one link each.

2. **The library share form asks for 5 to 6 decisions before you can post.** Where: `/library/new`: Title, One-line pitch, **Kind (select, 7)**, **Role (select, 10)**, Description, Files, More details (Or a link, How to install), **Who can see it (3 radios, + free-text "Companies, with commas between")**. What they face: 4 required fields plus "at least one of description, file, link or how to install", and no field says it is required. Errors come one at a time through the browser bubble; my first submit with a title alone failed on the pitch. Kind is a taxonomy a founder has to guess at (Skill vs Prompt vs Agent vs Automation vs Tool connection vs Playbook), and Role has 9 options, one of them "Founder's own work". For a Prompt, the form has no "paste your prompt" box: it goes in "Description", which the item page shows as "What it is". Why it stalls this persona: they want to drop in their setup in 30 seconds from a phone. Two required taxonomy pickers and a privacy choice make it a form to come back to later. **Harm 4.** Simpler default: Title + one "Paste it or attach it" box; pitch = the first line; Kind and Role optional or guessed; visibility stays "whole portfolio" with the other two choices under More details.

3. **Two composers for one win: Feed and Asks › Update.** Where: `/feed` "Post" (sheet: text + Ask/Win/Offer chips) and `/asks` (Ask/Update toggle + "Who are you trying to reach?"). Feed also has an "Ask" chip, while its pinned post says "asks for an intro still go through Asks." What they face: 2 places × 2 to 3 types for the same post. Why it stalls this persona: "where does a win go?" is a choice they shouldn't have to make, and a win posted in one place doesn't show in the other. **Harm 3.** Simpler default: one composer. A win/update goes to the Feed, and Asks only takes asks.

4. **The library, and the feed, sit four levels deep.** Where: tab bar (Rooms, Today, Asks, People, Inbox) → no library. You reach it through avatar → More → Library (2 taps, the 7th of 10 menu items), or Today's "More for you" row at the bottom. What they face: the persona's main reason to open the app is hidden in a menu next to Dark mode. **Harm 3.** Simpler default: a library card on Today ("3 new this week") or Library on the tab bar for portfolios that turn it on.

5. **The filters don't answer "what tools do others use?".** Where: `/library` Filter: Kind (7) + Role (10) + Show, plus All / Top / Yours. "All" (featured first, then upvotes) and "Top" (upvotes) gave the same order in the demo except for the featured item. What they face: 3 tabs + 17 filter options + a search box, and none of them is the AI tool or model (Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor...). Item pages don't say it either ("How to install: Add the skill."). Why it stalls this persona: they have to open item after item to learn the stack. **Harm 3.** Simpler default: one list (drop Top), one row of Kind chips, and a "Works with" line on each card.

6. **Notification settings are split over two pages with about 22 controls.** Where: avatar → Email (`/me/email`: 6 switches + Save) and avatar → Alerts (`/me/alerts`: 1 hour / Until tomorrow, timezone Change, Working hours / Evenings / Weekends / Custom, "Let through intro asks and messages", All alerts + 6 kinds, Muted people select with 20 names). What they face: two pages answering the same question, "what reaches me". **Harm 3** (they won't give up, but they'll skip it and get noise, or switch everything off). Simpler default: one "Notifications" page with one switch per channel and good defaults, and the rest under Advanced.

7. **The avatar menu has 10 items, and a member needs about 5.** Per item: My profile (needed), Alerts (merge with Email), Email (merge with Alerts), Your data (rare: a link at the foot of My profile), Jobs (useful to a hiring founder), Perks (occasional), Library (needed, see 4), Feed (needed, see 3/4), Dark mode (follow the system instead), Sign out (keep). **Harm 2.** Simpler default: Profile, Notifications, Library, Feed, Sign out, with the rest one level down.

8. **The weekly email asks the member to check a profile line they didn't ask about.** Where: digest item 3, "City: New York", with Right / Hide it / Fix it. What they face: 3 choices for data nobody asked about, in an email they opened for other reasons. **Harm 2.** Simpler default: show filled-in lines as right, with one "Fix my profile" link.

9. **The intro answer has 3 options where 2 would do.** Where: `/intros/<id>`: Yes, introduce them / Not this time / I don't know them well (both noes say "Maya is never told why") + Edit the note + Send. What they face: two kinds of "no" that do the same thing for the asker. **Harm 2.** Simpler default: Yes / No.

10. **Today keeps showing setup prompts.** Where: `/today`: the "Add Northwind Founders to your home screen" 3-step card (with "Hide this") and "Fill in your card from public profiles? Yes, fill it in / No, don't look me up". What they face: 2 extra decisions above the real content on every visit until they act. **Harm 2.** Simpler default: show once, then fold into My profile.

11. **My profile has 13 Edit links and 7 settings rows.** Where: `/me`: Name, Role, Company, City, Stage, Sector, Cohort or fund, Working on now, Can help with, Looking for, Tags, LinkedIn, Website (13 Edit links), then Privacy, Booking link, Alerts, My network, Our jobs, How you sign in (rows), plus the lookup Yes/No. **Harm 2.** Simpler default: one "Edit profile" button and 4 to 5 key lines shown.

## Visibly broken things (separate from the complexity findings)

- **My profile contradicts itself.** `/me` asks "Fill in your card from public profiles? Yes, fill it in / No, don't look me up" right above "Filled in from public profiles · refreshed 31 Oct", and City is marked "from public profiles". The same Yes/No appears on Today.
- **The secret scan only covers files.** `/library/new` says "Checked for keys and passwords" under Files. The Description and How-to-install text are not scanned (`src/modules/library/service.ts` runs `checkUploads(files)` only). A founder pasting a prompt with a key into Description gets no warning.
- **Members can't remove or edit their own ask or update.** On their own `/asks/<id>` a member gets only "Mark answered" (asks) or nothing (updates). Remove/Close are admin-only. Feed posts do have Delete. My test update "x" (`/asks/32edd571-30cb-4214-a130-8dbe1f623ce5`) is still up and I had no way to take it down. It goes when the demo resets tonight.
- **An update of one character is accepted.** "x" posted as an Asks update, with no minimum length.
- **The library's auto post to the Feed shows the raw URL.** Sharing a library item posts "Shared in the library: … (prompt)" with the link text `http://localhost:9563/library/<uuid>` "(opens in a new tab)", for an in-app page. Removing the item removes the post, which is right.
- **The library vanished mid-session for a while** (probably another tester switched the module off). `/library` showed a bare "That page isn't…" with "Go to the start", and Today's "More for you" and the menu's More lost Library and Perks with no word to the member. Low confidence that it's a bug; noting it in case "module off" should say so.
- **The intro "Answer" page's "Report this note"** sits right under Send. Not broken, but on a phone it's easy to tap by mistake.
