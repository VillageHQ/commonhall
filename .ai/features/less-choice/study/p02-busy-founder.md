## Findings

1. **Where:** `/asks?compose=1`, the "Who are you trying to reach? (optional)" toggle. **What Sam faced:** it is marked optional and folded away. Sam posted "An intro to a VP Procurement at a hospital network" without it, and got "Nobody has a path yet; it goes into Thursday's digest." Posting the same text with role and company filled gave "1 member can help" and an intro button. **Why it hurts this persona:** Sam skips anything marked optional and waits a week for the digest. All of the value is behind the field labelled as skippable. **Harm 5.** **Simpler default:** fill the role and company from the sentence ("VP Procurement" / "hospital network") and show them for a one-tap fix. Never say "optional" about the field that finds the path.

2. **Where:** `/asks` composer, "Post". **What Sam faced:** Sam already had an answered ask from 12 days ago, "intro to someone who runs procurement at a US hospital group". Priya introduced Alex Kim, Head of Procurement at Halcyon Health, through it. The composer gave no hint of this, and Sam posted twice (2 of the 5 weekly asks). The new ask matched Marcus, who knows Rosa Lindqvist, a CTO, rather than Priya, who knows Alex Kim. **Why it hurts this persona:** Sam forgets what he asked last month, spends his quota, and gets a worse match than he already had. **Harm 4.** **Simpler default:** before posting, show "You asked something like this on 8 Nov: Priya knows Alex Kim (Head of Procurement)" with "Ask Priya again" as the main button.

3. **Where:** Today "Post an ask" goes to `/asks?compose=1`. **What Sam faced:** he lands on the whole Asks page: the composer, the search box, 3 tabs and 8 feed cards (page 2037px). The input is one line, so his sentence is cut off at "An intro to a VP Procurement at a l". **Why it hurts this persona:** on a phone he cannot see what he typed, and the feed below pulls him into reading other people's asks. **Harm 3.** **Simpler default:** from Today, open a focused full-screen composer with a multi-line box and nothing else.

4. **Where:** `/asks` composer, the "Ask | Update" toggle. **What Sam faced:** 2 modes before typing. **Why it hurts this persona:** Sam came to ask, and "Update" is a second idea he has to rule out. **Harm 2.** **Simpler default:** Ask only. Move Update to a small link, or post as an update when the text has no request in it.

5. **Where:** `/intros/<id>`, answering "Marcus Webb asks you to introduce them to Rachel Kim". **What Sam faced:** 3 radio options with nothing pre-selected, and Send gives "Pick an answer." Picking Yes opens an empty "Rachel's email" field, which is required, so Sam has to leave the app to find it. Marcus' note, the reason to say yes, sits below the Send button, and a second copy of it is under "Edit the note". Then the status screen offers "Make a new link" and "I can't make this intro after all" and says "Nothing more to send; if you also reach them another way, a new link below works too." **Why it hurts this persona:** that is 4 decisions and a lookup to do a favour. He decides before reading the reason, and the follow-up copy makes him wonder whether he has to do more. **Harm 4** (the email lookup is where a phone user stops). **Simpler default:** show Marcus' note first and big "Yes, introduce" / "Not now" buttons. Pre-fill Rachel's email if it is known, otherwise ask "Paste Rachel's email or skip, and we give you a link to forward". Keep a single line, "Done. We'll tell you when Rachel answers", on the status screen.

6. **Where:** Today "You could help", "I can help", then `/asks/<id>`. **What Sam faced:** the first tap only opens the ask page, which has a second "I can help" button and "A note for Lena (optional)". So it takes 2 taps and 1 optional field for one decision. **Why it hurts this persona:** the button says it acts, but it does not. **Harm 2.** **Simpler default:** the Today button commits at once, with "Add a note" offered afterwards.

7. **Where:** Today "Not this week" on Amara's ask. **What Sam faced:** it says "Done. It won't show for you again.", which contradicts "this week". The ask still shows on `/asks?tab=for-you` with the "You could help" chip, and the tab still counts "For you 2". **Why it hurts this persona:** Sam can't tell whether he snoozed it or dismissed it for good, and the same ask comes back on another screen. **Harm 2.** **Simpler default:** label it "Not for me", and remove it from For you as well.

8. **Where:** Today, top of the page (second visit). **What Sam faced:** the order is the "LIVE NOW" room card with a big black Join, then the "Add Northwind Founders to your home screen" card with 3 numbered steps and a reference to Alerts, and only then "Waiting on you". The heading promises "2 things wait for you", but the 2 things are below 2 unrelated cards. **Why it hurts this persona:** he opens the app once a week and the first screen is a live talk and an install tutorial, not his items. **Harm 3.** **Simpler default:** put "Waiting on you" first, a one-line room strip after it, and the install hint at most once, then on Settings.

9. **Where:** Today "Waiting on you", "Your ask · 1 answer / Read" (second visit). **What Sam faced:** it was still listed after he had opened and read that ask. The new ask, which has a fresh match ("1 member can help: Marcus"), does not appear on Today, and the intro he just made (Marcus to Rachel) has no line either. **Why it hurts this persona:** Today repeats what he has done and leaves out what changed, so he stops trusting it to be his to-do list. **Harm 3.** **Simpler default:** clear "Read" once he opens the ask, and add "Marcus can introduce you: Ask" for new matches.

10. **Where:** `/asks/<own id>`, own ask page. **What Sam faced:** the box is labelled "Your answer" / "Write an answer" on his own ask. Priya appears twice, as the match card and as the answer author. "Ask Priya for an intro" is ambiguous: it doesn't say to whom, and Priya is the person who has done it. "Nobody has a path yet" shows twice on the empty ask (as the toast and in the body). **Why it hurts this persona:** he has to work out what each button would do. **Harm 2.** **Simpler default:** use "Reply" on your own ask. Use "Talk to Priya" when the helper is the target, and "Ask Priya to introduce you to Alex Kim" when there is a target.

11. **Where:** `/asks/<id>`, closing. **What Sam faced:** the only way to close is "Mark answered", including for a duplicate that nobody answered. There is no renew or extend, and nothing says whether open asks expire. Closing does not give back the weekly ask (still "2 of 5 left"). **Why it hurts this persona:** "answered" is a false statement for a duplicate, and he can't tell whether an old ask is still working for him. **Harm 2.** **Simpler default:** offer one "Close" (answered or not). Show "open until <date>" with a one-tap "Keep open", and give the quota back when an ask is closed with no answers.

12. **Where:** `/inbox`. **What Sam faced:** there are 2 tabs, "All" and "Intros". "All" shows only the 1 message and none of the 2 intros. The preview shows Sam's own last line ("Yes please. Thursday 4pm London works...") next to Priya's name and photo, so it reads as Priya's reply. The Intros row "Marcus Webb → Rachel Kim" has the status chip "with target", which is internal wording. **Why it hurts this persona:** "All" isn't all, and he can't tell whose turn it is. **Harm 2.** **Simpler default:** use one list that includes intros. Prefix the preview with "You:", and replace "with target" with "Waiting for Rachel".

13. **Where:** `/inbox/<id>` thread. **What Sam faced:** the thread has 4 controls besides Send (Report, Delete per message, Block), plus the banner "Replies by email land here too. Northwind Founders' admins can't read messages in the portal." **Why it hurts this persona:** this is minor. The banner is policy text on every visit. **Harm 1.** **Simpler default:** show the banner once.
