## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)

1. **A partner still cannot make an intro (finding 11).** Where: a member's profile (`/people/<id>`, Amara Diallo) and the ask page. What happened: the profile offers Message, "Ask for an intro via Amara", "Don't notify me about Amara's rooms", Block and "Manage this member". No "Introduce Amara to…". On the cement ask I could only say "I can help" and write that I *would* introduce her to a plant lead. The intro itself then has to happen outside the app. Harm 4. Simpler default: "Introduce to…" on the ask and the profile, with a member picker and double opt-in on both sides. (`p11-15-phone-profile`, `p11-13-phone-offered`)
2. **"1 intro waiting over 3 days" opens the full log (finding 12).** Where: Home "Needs you" link → `/admin/intros`. What happened: 19 rows, 5 state tabs, 3 periods and CSV. The waiting intro is not filtered or highlighted, and no row has a Nudge. On a phone that is a 2172px scroll to guess which row is late. Harm 4. Simpler default: the link opens just the late intro, with "Nudge [connector]". (`p11-10-phone-waiting`)
3. **The report still gives no verdict (findings 1 and 2).** Where: `/admin` and `/admin/report`. What happened: Home now links the report but still leads with chores (founders active, asks with no help, waiting intros, room joins). The report still has about 100 numbers in 9 blocks: headline, 3 tiles, outcomes, a 7-row funnel, connectors, an 8-row sector table, a cohort table, asks tiles. There is still no "vs last quarter" and no good/bad, so I still can't tell whether "5 made of 19" is good. On a phone it is 2965px, 3.5 screens. Harm 3. Simpler default: the headline plus the change since last quarter as Home's first line; fold the sector, cohort and connector tables under "Details". (`p11-02-report`, `p11-09-phone-report`)
4. **The console asks list still has no Help (finding 7).** Where: `/admin/asks`. What happened: Pin, Close and Remove on every row (28 to 34 buttons as asks were added), even in "No help yet". Helping is still on the ask's own page. Harm 3. Simpler default: a "No help yet" row opens the ask with the "I can help" box focused; move Pin/Close/Remove into a row menu.
5. **The magnifier chip on an ask still does nothing (finding 10).** Where: the ask page, chip "🔍 a plant manager in cement" (and "a founder who raised a seed round in New York"). What happened: I tapped it and nothing happened. It is not even a button to a screen reader. Harm 3. Simpler default: run that search in People, or drop the magnifier. (`p11-07-chip`)
6. **"1 member can help." still has no name (finding 9).** Where: the New York seed ask. What happened: still a bare count, with no link. An ask with nobody now says "Nobody has a path yet; anyone can help.", which is clearer. Harm 3. Simpler default: name the member and add "Introduce them". (`p11-18-phone-nyask`)
7. **"Asks" means two things in the report (finding 3).** Where: `/admin/report`. What happened: the headline says "19 asks and 5 intros" (intro requests). The bottom block says "10 asks posted" (board posts). Harm 2. Simpler default: "19 intro requests" in the headline.
8. **The email still differs from the screen (finding 4).** Where: the preview on `/admin/report/send`. What happened: "By what the founder needed" shows Customer, Hire, Investor and Expert all at 0, and Other at 19. The screen shows "By sector" with 8 real rows. Top connectors differ: the screen has Julia Weber and Amara Diallo, the email has neither. The email adds "An investor meeting: 0" and "Intros started from asks: 0 (0 made)". Harm 2. Simpler default: one breakdown in both places, with zero rows left out. (`p11-03-send`)
9. **After sending, the form stays live and doesn't name who got it (finding 5).** Where: `/admin/report/send`. What happened: "Sent to 2 partners." sits above both filled fields and a live "Send the report". Harm 2. Simpler default: replace the form with "Sent to dana.partner@…, lee.partner@…" and "Back to report". (`p11-04-sent`)
10. **NEW: the saved partner emails are shared by every admin.** Where: the "Partners' emails" field ("Saved for next time"). What happened: a few runs after I saved my two partners, the field held `partners@example.com`. Another admin's send had replaced it, and nothing said who saved it or when. Sent on a phone without a careful look, my report would go to their list. Harm 2. Simpler default: show "Last sent to … by …, on …" under the field, or save per admin. (`p11-19-phone-send`)
11. **Opening an ask still switches you to Founder view (finding 8).** Where: any ask opened from the console. What happened: a banner ("Founder view… You are Maya Ortiz, an admin", "Back to console"). On a phone the whole bottom tab bar also swaps from Home/Asks/Members/Announce/Set up to Rooms/Today/Asks/People/Inbox. Harm 2. Simpler default: no switch when the admin came from a console link. (`p11-12-phone-ask`)
12. **NEW: reply counts don't match.** Where: `/admin/asks` vs the ask page and the board. What happened: the console lists the cement ask and the New York ask at "2 replies". Each ask's page shows "1 answer", and the board says "1 answer" too. Harm 2. Simpler default: one count, with the same word ("answers") everywhere.
13. **"Declined 5" in the intro log vs "Declined 3, No answer 2" in the report (finding 14).** Unchanged. Harm 1. Simpler default: the report's split and words.
