## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)

1. **Two switches for Perks** (`/admin/perks`, "Show Perks to members"). I turned Perks off on Features. The Perks page switch still reads ON, and next to it Status says "Not yet: members don't see Perks". On Set up the Perks row says "optional", whatever the state. I can't tell which switch counts. Harm 3. Simpler: one switch, on Features. The Perks page holds only the embed code, and its row shows "on" or "off" (p10-06-perks-page-off).
2. **The time zone field** (Announce › Change the schedule › Time zone). It is still free text and defaults to UTC. "Berlin" is rejected with "Pick a time zone from the list.", but no list is visible. I had to know "Europe/Berlin". Harm 3. Simpler: fill in the browser's zone and offer a short select of common zones (p10-09-berlin-crop).
3. **NEW: Guided start sends me back to inviting** (Set up › Advanced › Guided start). The community launched long ago, but the row opens "Step 1 of 2, Invite founders" with a live invite form. Under "Profile fill-in: how did these members agree?" the selected answer is "Members agreed in our membership terms on 05/05/2026", while the other answer, "Ask each member at sign-in", carries the "Recommended" tag. The default is not the recommended answer, and it's a consent choice. Harm 3. Simpler: hide Guided start once its steps are done, and preselect the recommended answer (p10-14-guided).
4. **Mail says "Done" but isn't** (Set up › Mail row vs `/admin/mail`). The row says Done. The page says "1 mail check needs you" and "Inbound replies webhook: not set". Harm 3. Simpler: the row shows "1 check needs you" until every check passes (p10-12-mail).
5. **Advanced is "For engineers" but holds my settings** (`/admin/settings` › Advanced). It still hides Who can message a member, the data-protection contact, Short descriptor, From name, and the Perks, Job board, Flagged and Audit log rows. That is 8 rows, 7 fields and 2 selects. Harm 3. Simpler: keep only the sending domain, hosting and keys under Advanced, and show the feature rows in view (p10-02-advanced).
6. **Features has no descriptions** (`/admin/features`). It has 10 switches and 10 "i" buttons. Only Feed has a line ("Under More in the menu"). The names still don't match the member menu (Inbox = Messages + Intros). Harm 2. Simpler: a one-line description under each switch (p10-03-features).
7. **The Weekly email row lands on the wrong tab.** The row opens Announce on the "Announcements" tab, with the schedule under the announcement form. The tab named "Weekly email" still has no schedule. Harm 2. Simpler: put the schedule on the Weekly email tab and open that tab (p10-08-weekly, p10-10-digest).
8. **The preview opens on me, not a founder** (`/admin/digest`, Member select). It opens on "Maya Ortiz". The 21 names show no role, and "A made-up member" is last. I picked Sam Lee by guessing. The card under the schedule also previews Maya. Harm 2. Simpler: open on a founder who has items, or on the made-up member (p10-10-digest).
9. **Village shows setup steps while it is connected** (`/admin/village`). This is NEW in how it looks. "Connected" sits above "1. Create a developer account, 2. Finish Village's short checklist, 3. Open your keys page", with 2 empty key fields and Test the connection. It reads as if I should paste keys again. Advanced still has 2 locked "always on" switches and 1 greyed select. Harm 2.
