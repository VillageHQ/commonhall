## 2. What got simpler

- **Finding 1, 2, 3:** Start is one button with one line under it: "Starts now and alerts everyone." The Notify switch,
  the three audiences, Invite only, Now/Schedule and the two "Everyone"s are all gone. It's 2 taps from the tab bar
  to a live room that tells everyone (p05-01-rooms, p05-02-start-tapped).
- **Finding 6, 5:** past the 3 alerting starts a day, the room tells me once: "People weren't alerted this time: 3 rooms
  a day alert everyone." It does this for a one-tap start (p05-11-quiet-start) and for a scheduled room I start early
  (p05-12-scheduled-start-at-limit). Before, the scheduled case was skipped silently.
- **Finding 8, 4, 9:** "Tell people I'm here" and its 10-name picker are gone. So are "People I pick", which pushed
  Start under the tab bar, and invite-only rooms that used up a notify while reaching nobody.
- **Finding 12, 11:** a receiver turns room alerts off with one switch at the top of Rooms. On the Alerts page, the two
  room switches are now one, "Room alerts". Muted people sits folded. Scheduled cards show only "Remind me"; the
  "Following" bell is gone.
- **Finding 13, 10:** others see my live room as "SL · Sam is in" (p05-13-admin-sees-card). As the host, I can rename a
  live or scheduled room in place.

## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)

1. **NEW. The alert goes out before I can name the room.** Where: Rooms › Start a room, then the room's Rename.
   What happened: one tap starts the room and sends the alert at once. The room's name at that moment is "Sam's room",
   and the code reads the title when it sends. So everyone gets "Sam Lee started “Sam's room”". My rename to "What
   closed our biggest deal" comes too late for the one alert that brings people in. Nothing on Rooms tells me this
   before I tap. Harm 3.
   Simpler default: open the room with the name field focused, and hold the alert about 20 s or until I Save,
   whichever comes first. Or put an optional "What's it about?" line above Start that is never required.
2. **The host can't end a room whose audio fails, and the error is jargon.** Where: the room page after about 12 s.
   What happened: "could not establish pc connection", and only "Back to Rooms". There is no End room, no Try again,
   and no Mute. The room stays on Rooms as "Live now · 1 in" with Join until it ends itself (p05-04-renamed,
   p05-05-rooms-mine-live). This is the same gap as before, in a new form. Harm 3.
   Simpler default: say "We couldn't connect your audio" with Try again. Always give the host End room, even when
   not connected.
3. **No word that anyone was told** (finding 7). Where: the room page after a start that did alert. What happened: no
   line and no count. Only a quiet start says anything, so silence means "it worked", and I have to know that. The
   app records how many people the alert reached but doesn't show it. Maya's alert log showed no room alert from any
   of my starts (p05-14-admin-sent-log). Demo mode delivers nothing, and her day's cap may have been used up, so I
   couldn't confirm anyone was reached. Harm 3.
   Simpler default: one line on the room, "Everyone was alerted (14 people)."
4. **NEW in form: at the limit, Rooms still promises "Starts now and alerts everyone."** Where: Rooms, under Start a
   room, on my 4th start of the day. What happened: I only learn after starting that nobody was told. The line gives
   no time when alerts come back, and before the limit nothing shows how many are left. Three quick starts use up the
   day: I used all three in about 5 minutes on rooms I ended at once. A mis-tap now costs a real alert. Harm 3.
   Simpler default: at the limit, change the line to "Starts quietly: 3 alerting rooms a day, back tomorrow at 7:05
   PM". Don't count a room that is ended within a minute with nobody in it.
5. **A scheduled room can't be cancelled or moved** (finding 10). Where: /rooms/<id> for "Deal debrief part 2" (p05-09-scheduled-page).
   What happened: the only controls are Rename and Start now. The schedule form doesn't say whether the room starts by
   itself at its time or waits for me, or that starting it alerts everyone. Harm 2.
   Simpler default: a "Cancel room" link. One line on the form: "At that time we remind you to start it; starting
   alerts everyone."
6. **NEW, small. "Start a room" quietly means "back to your room" while mine is live.** Where: Rooms. What happened:
   the button and its "alerts everyone" line stay the same, but tapping reopens my live room and alerts nobody. That's
   the right behaviour with the wrong words. Harm 1.
   Simpler default: while I host a live room, the button reads "Back to your room".
7. **The host sees a greyed "Raise hand" while connecting** (from before). Where: the room bar. Harm 1.
   Simpler default: show the host's Mute and End room greyed out instead.
8. **The Alerts page is still long for a receiver** (finding 12, rest). Where: /me/alerts. What happened: Pause (2),
   time zone Change, 4 quiet-time presets, the "Let through" switch, 6 kind switches, Muted people and the log. It's
   fine now that the room switch also lives on Rooms. Harm 1.
