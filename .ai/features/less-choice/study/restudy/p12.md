## 2. What got simpler

- **Finding 2 (share form).** One box now: "Paste it, link it or attach a file". Title + paste + Share = 2 taps, no
  required pickers. Kind is guessed and shown as a line I can change. A missing paste gets a message in words at the
  top, and my title stays.
- **Broken list (secret scan).** The paste is now checked. A fake key on line 2 got "The pasted text, line 2, looks
  like an AI provider key. Take it out and share again. Nothing was saved." My text stayed in the box.
- **Finding 11 and the broken "fill in your card" contradiction.** My profile has one "Edit card" instead of 13 Edit
  links. The "Fill in your card from public profiles? Yes / No" question is gone from My profile.
- **Finding 10.** Today no longer shows the home-screen card or the lookup Yes/No above the content.
- **Findings 9 and 3 (part).** The intro answer is now two buttons (Introduce / No thanks) instead of 3 radios plus
  Send (read from the built page; not walked live). On Asks the Ask / Update toggle is gone: you type, then "Post" or
  "Post a win instead", and a win doesn't use one of the 5 weekly asks.

## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)

1. **The weekly email still has nothing from the library** (finding 1). Where: Sam's weekly email (`/admin/digest`).
   What happened: a profile check ("City: New York: Right / Hide it / Fix it"), one ask in motion, the portal link.
   Nothing about what other founders use. The library sends a "New in the library" phone alert (Alerts page), but
   the email, which is how I come in, has no such line. Harm 4. Simpler default: "New in the library this week: top
   3", one link each.
2. **Two places for one win, with two names** (finding 3). Where: `/feed` Post sheet (Ask / Win / Offer chips) and
   `/asks` "Post a win instead". What happened: my win in Asks said "Update posted." and shows with an "Update"
   label. It never reached the Feed. The Feed still has an "Ask" chip, while its pinned post says intro asks go
   through Asks. Harm 3. Simpler default: one place for wins (the Feed). Asks takes only asks, and "Post a win
   instead" posts to the Feed.
3. **The library is still buried, now deeper in the menu** (finding 4). Where: avatar menu. What happened: Library is
   the 12th of 15 items. On a phone the list scrolls inside the menu and Feed is cut in half by the line above Dark
   mode (`p12-menu`). Today only has the small "More for you: Jobs, Perks, Library" row at the foot. Harm 3. Simpler
   default: a "New in the library" card on Today, or Library on the tab bar when it is on.
4. **NEW: the avatar menu grew from 10 to 15 items** (finding 7). Where: avatar menu. What happened: 9 Settings rows,
   the same 9 rows again at the foot of My profile, and "Our jobs" (Settings) next to "Jobs" (More). Harm 2.
   Simpler default: My card, Notifications, Library, Feed, Sign out; the other settings live on My card only.
5. **Notifications are still two pages** (finding 6). Where: `/me/email` (6 switches + Save) and `/me/alerts` (1 hour,
   Until tomorrow, timezone Change, 4 quiet-time buttons, Let through, All alerts + 6 kinds, a 20-name mute list).
   What happened: unchanged. The library is on Alerts but not on Email. Harm 3. Simpler default: one Notifications
   page, one switch per channel, the rest under Advanced.
6. **The library filters still don't answer "what tools do others use?"** (finding 5). Where: `/library`. What
   happened: the same 3 tabs and Kind (7) + Role (10) + Show. "Top" gave the same order as "All". Cards don't say
   which AI tool they work with. Harm 2. Simpler default: one list, a row of Kind chips, a "Works with" line.
7. **I still can't take down my own ask or update** (broken list before). Where: my own `/asks/<id>`. What happened:
   my test win `/asks/c2875064-4381-4a16-b999-c2d25ad2fea9` has only a reply box. My ask has only "Mark answered".
   Feed posts and library items can be removed. Harm 2. Simpler default: "Remove" on your own ask or update.
8. **The weekly email still asks me to check a profile line** (finding 8). Where: email item 1, "City: New York" with
   Right / Hide it / Fix it. Harm 2. Simpler default: one "Fix my profile" link.
9. **NEW: a one-line prompt shows twice, and there is no Copy.** Where: a newly shared library item. What happened:
   with the pitch left empty, the pitch is the first line, so the same sentence shows under the title and under
   "What it is". A prompt has no Copy button; I have to select the text by hand on a phone. Harm 2. Simpler default:
   show the pitch only when it differs; add "Copy" on a prompt.
10. **NEW: words that don't match.** The menu says "My card", but the Email and Alerts pages go back to "My profile".
    "Post a win instead" ends in "Update posted." The email's intro links say "Not this time", but the app says "No
    thanks". Harm 1. Simpler default: one word for each thing.
