## 2. What got simpler

- **Finding 1 and 2:** "Add a job" is now the first form on /me/jobs: Title, Location, "Apply link or email (optional)",
  and "Leave it empty and members apply by messaging you." The hiring-tool select is folded under "Already on Greenhouse,
  Lever…? Paste your careers link". An email address works. A job with no link gets an Apply that opens a message to me with
  "I'd like to apply for Founding backend engineer at Tallyhealth." filled in (seen as Maya, p08-14-apply-as-other).
- **Finding 3:** a job I type goes on the existing Tallyhealth card, marked "added by hand", with its own bin button. No
  second "Tallyhealth" card, and the broken "Added by hand has no public job feed" sentence is gone (p08-03-added).
- **Finding 7:** the button now says "Post a hiring request · uses no weekly ask", the composer repeats it, and it is true:
  the count stayed at 3 of 5 after the request and dropped to 2 only after my normal ask. My head-of-sales ask was not blocked.
- **Finding 8:** the Ask/Update switch is gone. The composer has one field, Post, and a small "Post a win instead".
  "Who are you trying to reach?" is now a closed "Name who you're trying to reach" line.
- **Finding 12:** Today says "Nothing needs you right now" instead of promising one thing and showing six buttons. On the
  phone the whole page fits one screen, so Jobs and Perks under "More for you" are visible without scrolling (p08-09-today).

## 3. What still makes me hesitate

1. **Find candidates still doesn't find candidates (finding 6).** Where: /search/candidates from a job's "Find candidates".
   What happened: for "Founding backend engineer in Boston" I got Alex Kim (Head of Procurement) and Rosa Lindqvist (another
   company's CTO). For "Head of sales (hospitals)" I got the same two plus Dana Morris (VP Supply Chain at a hospital). These
   are buyers and other founders' executives, not people for the role. Harm 4. Simpler default: match on the role's title and
   skills; when nothing fits, say "No candidates in reach yet" and leave "Post a hiring request" as the one button.
2. **NEW: the hiring request doesn't show the job.** Where: the posted request, seen by another member (p08-17-request-as-other).
   What happened: the button promised "A hiring request … with the job's link", but other members see only the sentence. There's
   no job link, no location and no "Hiring" mark, so it reads like any other ask. After I removed the job, the request stayed
   up with nothing to point to. Harm 3. Simpler default: show the job as a card under the request (title, location, Apply),
   and close the request when the job is removed.
3. **Too many equal "can help" buttons, weak matches (finding 10).** Where: the posted ask page, both the request and the
   ask. What happened: 5 people × "Ask X for an intro" + "Message" = 10 buttons. For the Boston engineer: London, Netherlands
   and Berlin hiring. For hospital sales: referral programmes, hotels, legal teams, farmers in Spain, CFOs. None sold to
   hospitals. Harm 3. Simpler default: show the 1 or 2 best matches with one button each; put the rest behind "More people".
4. **Perks unchanged (finding 11).** Where: /perks on the phone (p08-10-perks). What happened: 6 Claim buttons, no terms, two
   titles cut ("50% off bookkeeping for 6 m…", "20% off a year of support sof…"). Claim opens an outside tab, and the app
   shows no code or steps and keeps no record that I claimed. Harm 3. Simpler default: one line of terms under each title;
   Claim opens a sheet with the code or steps first.
5. **The ask route and the job route still don't know about each other (finding 9).** Where: the /asks composer. What
   happened: I typed "Looking for a head of sales…" and nothing offered to make it a job or a free hiring request. So the
   ask cost me one of my 5, when the job route (Today → Jobs → Add yours → 3 fields → Add → Find candidates → Post a hiring
   request) would have cost none. Harm 2. Simpler default: when an ask names a role, offer "Post this as a job (free hiring
   request)".
6. **Jobs is still not in the nav (finding 4).** Where: the sidebar and bottom bar (Rooms, Today, Asks, People, Inbox). What
   happened: I reach Jobs only through Today's "More for you" or the URL. /me/jobs still has "My profile" as its back link.
   On /search/candidates the sidebar highlights People. Harm 2. Simpler default: show Jobs in the nav when my company has a job.
7. **The scope toggle changes nothing (finding 5).** Where: /search/candidates, "In our network" / "Everywhere". What
   happened: the same 2 people both ways ("2 people in reach" vs "2 people everywhere"). Harm 2. Simpler default: one list,
   with network paths first.
8. **The "Close" chip (finding 6, broken list).** Where: next to each path on /search/candidates (p08-05-candidates).
   What happened: it still looks like a dismiss button but means a close tie. Harm 2. Simpler default: "close tie" in words,
   or drop the chip.
9. **NEW: removing a job is one tap with no undo, next to a company-wide Remove.** Where: /me/jobs. What happened: the bin
   icon removed my job at once, with no confirm or undo message. Just below the list, a bare "Remove" next to "Hide this
   company from the board" removes the whole company card. Harm 2. Simpler default: show "Removed. Undo" after a job is
   removed; label the company button "Remove Tallyhealth from the board" and ask before doing it.
10. **My own pulled jobs still say "Apply" (broken list).** Where: /jobs (p08-04-board). What happened: my typed jobs show
    only "Find candidates" (good), but the two pulled Tallyhealth jobs still show both "Find candidates" and "Apply". Harm 1.
    Simpler default: hide Apply on your own company's jobs.
