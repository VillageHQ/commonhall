## 2. What got simpler

- **Finding 2:** "Booking link" is now the 5th item in the avatar menu's Settings list (`p07-menu`), 2 taps from any
  page. On /me it's the 5th row under a "Settings" heading, not buried below 13 Edit links.
- **Finding 5:** The People header went from 7 controls to 3 (search, Filter, "Mentors and experts"). The 4 intent
  chips are gone. On a phone, "Book a call" sits right on each mentor's card (`p07-people`).
- **Finding 9:** Share in the library went from 7 decisions to 2 fields and one button (`p07-library-new`). I don't
  have to classify anything twice.
- **Finding 7:** The Asks composer is one field plus Post. The win toggle, "Name who you're trying to reach" and the
  quota only appear once I type, and the quota reads as words ("2 of 5 asks left this week"), with no empty circles.
- **Finding 3 (in part):** After a refused save, the booking field keeps what I typed, so I fix it instead of
  retyping it.

## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)

1. **/me/booking → /people?view=experts. Saving a link still doesn't list me as a mentor (finding 1).** I saved a
   link and Sam was not among the 5. During the walk Noah Berg appeared as "Mentor", because an admin set it. A mentor
   has no control to list themselves and no sign they're missing. **Harm 4.** Simpler default: anyone with a booking
   link shows under "Mentors and experts", or saving the link offers "List me as a mentor", ticked.
2. **NEW. /people search box, "mentor".** The search said "No member matches / Nobody in the community matches those
   words" and offered "Search their networks for 'mentor'" (`p07-search-mentor`), even though 3 members carry a
   "Mentor" badge. A founder who types the word instead of tapping the link is told there are none. **Harm 3.** Simpler
   default: search matches community roles ("mentor", "advisor", "expert"), or a no-match on those words points to
   Mentors and experts.
3. **/me/booking, Booking link field. `cal.com/demo-mentor` is still refused** with "Use a full https:// address…"
   (`p07-booking-short`) (finding 3). **Harm 2.** Simpler default: add `https://` when it's missing.
4. **/people/<mentor> action row. "Ask for an intro" is still the black primary button, and "Book a call" is
   secondary** (`p07-grace`) (finding 4). Follow became "Don't notify me about Grace's rooms". **Harm 2.** Simpler
   default: when a member has a booking link, make "Book a call" the primary button.
5. **/asks/<id>, "I can help". A helper's booking link still isn't attached** (finding 8). Sam's own "Can help" answer
   shows only the note, with no "Book a call". The asks code never reads the booking link. **Harm 2.** Simpler
   default: add "Book a call with <name>" to the answer and its email when the helper has a link.
6. **/people/<me>. My own profile view still never shows my "Book a call"** (`p07-own-profile`), so I can't check that
   the link I saved is live. **Harm 2.** Simpler default: show it on my own view, with a "Members see this" note.
7. **/people?view=experts, Filter panel (finding 5, in part).** The view link now reads "Everyone" next to the
   mentor's cap icon. It names where it goes, not where I am. Opening Filter shows a second "Everyone" plus an
   "Experts and mentors" tab that repeats it, and the button says "Filter · 1" though I picked no filter. The 5
   dropdowns (45 options) are still there (`p07-filter`). **Harm 2.** Simpler default: drop the tabs from Filter. In
   the experts view, show "Mentors and experts ✕" and keep only Role.
8. **/asks. The page still opens on the composer and the "Open" tab, not on "For you"** (finding 7, the rest).
   **Harm 1.** Simpler default: open on "For you" when it has items.
9. **/library list. Upvoted buttons are still solid black** like primary actions (`p07-library`) (finding 10).
   **Harm 1.** Simpler default: a quiet filled icon plus the count.

Not verified: finding 6 (asks I could help with on Today). The code shows a "You could help" section with inline
