## 2. What got simpler

- **#1 One settings list.** The avatar menu and /me show the same 9 pages in the same order: My card, Privacy, Alerts,
  Email, Booking link, Your network, Our jobs, Your data, How you sign in. On /me each row says where it stands
  ("Shown to members", "Not added", "2 open jobs"). The alert log is now linked from Alerts as "What was sent and
  skipped".
- **#3 Quiet means quiet.** With Evenings added and the let-through switch off, DMs and intro asks are held in the
  evening too. That took 2 fewer taps and removed the trap from the first study.
- **#2 Intro asks have one switch.** Network now says "Intro asks to you are switched on Privacy" and links there. Its
  own switch is about something else ("members can ask you to introduce people you know"), so the two no longer use the
  same sentence.
- **#6 and #13 Fewer redundant choices.** "Who can message me" has 2 options instead of 3. "Edit card" opens all 13
  fields with one Save.
- **#9 (part) and broken #3: no contradiction on /me.** The "Fill in your card from public profiles? Yes / No" prompt is
  gone, and so are Hide my past roles and Correct. Only "Hide the city from public profiles" is left on the card.

## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)

1. **Alerts and Email are still two separate pages** (finding #4, #10). Where: /me/alerts and /me/email. What happened:
   6 email switches with different names for the same events ("Messages" vs "When someone messages me"). Neither page
   links to the other. Email has no quiet hours, so "nothing in the evening" still can't be done for email. The digest
   is still "Thursdays at 09:00 UTC". Email needs Save while Alerts saves on each tap. Harm 4. Simpler default: one
   "Notify me about" list with Push and Email columns and one quiet time for both, with times in my own time zone.
2. **Hiding still leaves me unsure whether intros reach me** (finding #5). Where: /me/privacy, "Nobody" and "Let members
   ask me for intros". What happened: "Nobody" now says "Members won't find you in People or be pointed to you on
   asks". The intro switch stays on with "You approve each one" and nothing about how anyone can still ask. As Maya,
   searching "Sam" in People found no member, so the direct way to ask me is gone. Harm 3 (was 4). Simpler default:
   under Nobody, one line saying what still works, e.g. "Members can still ask you to introduce people you know through
   your shared network; nobody can open your profile to ask you directly."
3. **Public-profile lookup is on by default, and its off switch is on another page** (finding #9). Where: /me "Filled in
   from public profiles · refreshed 1 Nov" and "Hide the city…"; Your data, "Don't look me up on public profiles". What
   happened: my card was filled in without my asking. The consent record says I "agreed in its membership terms". The
   off switch lives on Your data, not next to the filled-in card. Harm 3. Simpler default: a "Stop looking me up" link
   next to the "Filled in from public profiles" line on /me.
4. **NEW: intro asks are on, but I never said yes.** Where: Your data › Your consent record, "Members can ask you for
   intros: not answered: members can ask you". What happened: Privacy shows the switch on, while the record says I never
   answered. Harm 2. Simpler default: show "On (you haven't chosen yet)" on Privacy, or ask once.
5. **Time zone and a 419-option list** (finding #8). Where: Alerts › Quiet times, "Asia/Beirut Change". What happened:
   the zone comes from the browser, which is right if my phone is right. But Change still opens a select with 419
   options. Harm 2. Simpler default: a short list of common zones, with "Other…" for the rest.
6. **The let-through switch shows before any quiet time exists** (finding #3, the rest of it). Where: Alerts › Quiet
   times. What happened: with no quiet time set, the switch is still there and doesn't say it only matters during quiet
   times. Harm 2. Simpler default: show it only once a quiet time exists, as "During quiet times, still let through…".
7. **"Credit me in reports" is on and filed under Your data** (finding #11). Where: /me/data › Your switches. What
   happened: unchanged. Harm 2. Simpler default: move it to Privacy and make it off until chosen.
8. **"All alerts" next to the kind switches** (finding #7). Where: Alerts › What alerts you. What happened: it's still
   unclear whether the master switch wins, and every kind starts on. Harm 2. Simpler default: "Sent to me" on and "From
   the portfolio" off for new members; drop the master switch, since Pause already covers it.
9. **Two places to change my LinkedIn URL** (finding #12). Where: the LinkedIn field under /me Edit card, and /me/network
   "Change my LinkedIn profile". Harm 2. Simpler default: edit it on the card only; Network shows it read-only.
10. **NEW: the avatar menu is longer.** Where: the avatar menu. What happened: 15 items now (9 Settings, 4 More, Dark
    mode, Sign out). On a phone it scrolls, with Sign out pinned at the foot. "Feed" sits right on the foot's rule
    (`p06-menu`). Harm 1. Simpler default: fine as it is. Maybe show Settings as one row that opens the list.
