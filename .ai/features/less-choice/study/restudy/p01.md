## 2. What got simpler
- **Finding 1 (Village first, harm 4):** Village is gone from the guided start. It is now 2 steps, Invite founders
  then Your card. Village sits on Set up as "Network search … Optional." That also removes the wrong
  "Connect Village (done)".
- **Finding 2 (no community name):** `/setup` now asks for "Community name" with a hint saying founders see it on their
  invite. Alex's invite said "Join Northwind Founders" and his welcome said "Welcome to Northwind Founders".
- **Findings 4, 5 and 11 (consent question, 3 send buttons, link only readable as text):** The invite step has
  one box, "Create invite links" and the line "Mail is off: you get a link to copy for each founder". The
  consent question is gone when Village is off. Each founder gets their own row with "Copy" (it turns into "Copied").
- **Findings 6, 7 and 8 (second checklist, logo blocking launch, mail that can't be finished):** After "Save and
  finish", Home shows "This week" with no checklist. The Set up badge counted down 3 → 1 → gone, and the logo and
  mail no longer block anything.
- **Findings 16 and 19 (fill-in switch, chips that do nothing):** The privacy step no longer has the profile fill-in
  switch, and the "A customer / A hire …" chips are gone from People.

## 3. What still makes me hesitate (worst first)
1. **`/admin/members`, "Waiting for your OK (1)" → "Yes, this is them" / "Wrong person?"** (finding 9, unchanged).
   Alex accepted the link I made, but he shows as "Needs a look", and Home says "1 person waiting for your OK". The
   text "anyone holding the link could have done that" still alarms me. At 40 founders that is 40 more clicks.
   Harm 3. Simpler: trust whoever accepts a link the admin made, or offer "Confirm all".
2. **`/setup`, "Setup token"** (finding 3, unchanged). I still have to get a secret from whoever installed the app.
   `/setup?token=…` doesn't fill the field. "Where to find it" opens an outside guide in a new tab. On the empty
   copy, the token I was given was rejected outright as "too weak". A platform lead would be stuck until the engineer
   redeploys. Harm 3. Simpler: the installer prints a one-time `/setup?token=…` link that fills the field.
3. **`/admin/links`, "Copy"** (finding 10, now warned but unchanged). "Copy makes a new link; the one you sent before
   stops working." If I copy Alex's link again here to resend it, the link I already sent him dies. Harm 3.
   Simpler: Copy returns the live link, and "New link" is a separate button.
4. **`/admin/settings` (Set up)** (finding 12, mostly unchanged). There are still 3 Saves on one page ("Save settings",
   "Save", "Save"), and "Asks a week" (5) and "Outcome question" (14 days) are in plain view. The plan select is
   gone from view. NEW: the Mail row says "Not yet" although the setup badge already counted mail as done
   ("you copy the links"). Harm 2. Simpler: one Save; move the numbers under Advanced; have the Mail row say
   "Off: you copy links".
5. **`/admin/mail`** (finding 8, page unchanged). It still says "2 mail checks need you", and still shows "Check DNS
   now" (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MX) and a webhook URL, with 0 fields I can fill. It no longer blocks setup, but it still
   reads as broken. Harm 2. Simpler: "Optional: ask your engineer to add Resend", and hide the checks until a key is
   set.
6. **`/welcome` (Alex)** (finding 14, unchanged). It says "Two questions" but shows 6 boxes. Both questions are
   required ("Answer both questions; a line each is enough.") and can't be skipped. Harm 2. Simpler: show only the 2
   questions and put Role/Company/City behind "Add more".
7. **`/today` (Alex)** (finding 18, half fixed). "Post an ask" is now the clear action. But "Almost there: Your admin is
   still setting things up. Network search starts once an admin connects it." still tells founders the product is
   unfinished. The 3-step "Add to home screen" guide is also still there. Harm 2. Simpler: drop the admin-state card
   for members.
8. **`/people/<jordan>`, "Ask for an intro"** (finding 20, unchanged). It is still offered next to "Jordan hasn't
   shared a network yet." Harm 2. Simpler: hide it until the person has a network.
9. **`/admin/features`** (finding 13, mostly unchanged). There are still 10 switches, and their descriptions are behind
   10 "About …" buttons. Network is on although Village isn't connected. (Rooms now says "Needs a voice server" and
   Feed says "Under More in the menu", which helps.) Harm 2. Simpler: a one-line description under each switch;
   tie Network to Village.
10. **`/admin/start?step=card`** (finding 15, unchanged). The admin's card still uses founder examples ("a claims
    tool for mid-size insurers…"). Harm 1. Simpler: examples written for a platform lead.
11. **`/welcome` and `/welcome/privacy` on a phone** (finding 17, unchanged). The tab bar (Today, Asks, People,
    Inbox) is shown during onboarding. On `/welcome` it covers the Company field as the page scrolls (shot
    `p01-a-welcome`). Harm 1. Simpler: hide the tab bar during welcome.
12. **`/people` (Alex)** (finding 19, mostly fixed). With 2 members there are still "Filter" and a NEW
    "Mentors and experts" button above the list. Harm 1. Simpler: hide both below about 10 members.
13. **Dead-link page** (finding 11, page text unchanged). A mistyped token still says "That link doesn't work any
    more… Ask whoever invited you for a new invite" (shot `p01-dead-link`). It doesn't say whether the link was
    mistyped or replaced. It matters less now that links are copied, not read. Harm 1.
