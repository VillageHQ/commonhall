## Closing answers

**The tabs I'd want (in order)**
1. **Home**: one "What do you need?" box at the top, then "Waiting on you" (intro yes/no, asks I can help with) as short cards.
2. **Ask**: my asks plus the community's open asks, with intros as one kind of ask.
3. **People**: Search and Directory as one box.
4. **Inbox**: messages AND intro threads together (an intro is a conversation).
5. **Rooms**, only if live rooms become a real habit (see below).

Everything else goes under the avatar: My profile (card plus Edit), Settings (notifications, privacy, data), Jobs, Perks, Library, Feed. Book a call goes on my profile.

**What I'd never use (honestly)**
Feed (I can't even say how it differs from "Updates" on Asks), Library and its leaderboard ("Monthly investor update drafter"), Perks on the home screen, the Directory filters (stage/sector/city/cohort/"Updated: any time"), "Muted broadcasts", Dark mode, the "Filled in from public profiles" Correct/Hide lines, the job board sync block on my profile, and "I'm available to talk" from my inbox.

**Rooms**
Maybe, as a listener in the car. That's the one place a live audio room fits me better than reading. What would make me join: a specific topic and a named person I trust ("Priya on hiring a first VP Sales, 15 min, now"), and one tap from the alert straight into listening, muted by default. I'd never start one myself. Notify others: no, not from me. If someone else's room alerts me, it should only come from people I've had an intro or conversation with, or on topics I chose. Never "the whole community" (which is what "I'm available to talk" does today: "alerts the community").

**Quiet hours**
Don't alert me 9-6 on weekdays except for direct messages and an intro waiting on me, and never in the evenings or on weekends. Also don't alert me with broadcasts or rooms by default. I'd look under my avatar, in one "Notifications" page at the top. Today it's split between "Email" and "Alerts", and quiet hours sits halfway down Alerts with no times visible.

**My top 5 problems (worst first)**
1. **today / ask**: "Post an ask" leads to an intro wizard ("What do you need? A customer / A hire ...") while the real free-text box sits on the Asks tab. I can't tell where to ask for help, and that is the one thing I came for.
2. **intros_[id]**: Saying yes takes 3 radio choices, typing "Rachel's email, if you say yes", and "Send my answer". On a phone, that means "later", which means never.
3. **inbox / inbox_[id]**: "I'm available to talk ... alerts the community" sits as the main button above my messages. It's one mis-tap away from broadcasting myself to everyone.
4. **me / me_data**: 529 and 526 words, 52 controls, "Share my network / Stop sharing" on three screens, Dark mode under "Your data". Settings sprawl I'll never untangle.
5. **today**: Too long, and it leads with "You could help with these 2". About 9 sections (Perks, Jobs, library, new members) bury my own ask ("Your asks in motion") in fifth place. Repeats like "Nobody is introduced without saying yes" (on 9 screens) and "4 of 5 asks left this week" (on 5) add reading and no answers.
