# Rafael Costa: walkthrough

Repeat founder, climate hardware, pre-seed. Phone only for this. Notifications mostly off. My test: show me in 10 seconds
why this beats the WhatsApp group I already have with 40 founders. WhatsApp gives me one screen, one box and the
people. Anything more has to earn its place.

## F2: drop into a live room

  chores. "Sam's week at Northwind Founders" reads like a newsletter. "Each item also works by replying to Thursday's

## Closing answers

**Tabs I'd want (phone, in order):**
1. Rooms (live now first, then coming up)
2. Asks (the one-line composer and the board)
3. People (Search and Directory as one, with "Message" / "Ask for an intro")
4. Inbox (messages and intros together)
5. Me (profile, alerts, email, data)

Today becomes the top of Rooms or Asks rather than a tab. Feed merges into Asks (it's "updates" anyway). Jobs, Perks
and Library go into a "More" sheet or onto the People / Me pages. "Is this right?" profile checks happen once at
sign-up.

**Never:** Library (I'm not sharing prompts on a leaderboard), Feed, Perks after one look, the Directory filters
(cohort, "Fresh as of"), Muted broadcasts, "I'm available to talk".

**Rooms:** Yes, if three things are true. It's live now, on a topic I care about (fundraising for hardware, supply
chains), and people I respect are already in it, with their faces showing. A card reading "0 in the room" means I
won't join. A short scheduled slot ("Thursday 5pm, 30 min") beats random pop-ups. Notify others: yes when I start a
room, never just for joining one. Send it to people I chose or who follow that topic, never to the whole community.
"Alerts the community" is exactly why people end up muting each other.

**Alerts:** never during working hours (9 to 6 is lab and factory time), never evenings after 9pm, never weekends.
Alert me only for: someone answered my ask, an intro waiting for me, a direct message, and a room starting on one of my
topics. I'd look for this in a bell or gear icon at the top, or under Me > Alerts. I only found it via the "My
profile" back link. Quiet hours need real times showing, not a bare toggle.

**Top 5 problems (worst first):**
1. Today and the phone tab bar: Rooms isn't there. The word "room" doesn't appear on Today. Rooms only shows as the
   footer link "Rooms Library" under "That's everything this week."
2. Rooms: the card shows "0 in the room" and has no Join button, and the page opens with two broadcast buttons and the
   fine print "Opens a room with you as host and alerts the community…".
3. Joined room: no sound until a second tap ("Your browser paused the sound. Tap to hear the room"), with "ON STAGE"
   empty and "0 LISTENING". I'd leave.
4. Today: "Post an ask" lands on an intro wizard ("What do you need? A customer / A hire / An investor / An expert")
   instead of the one-line composer on Asks. Two buttons, two flows, one job.
5. Everywhere: the quota sentence "Asks you post and intro asks share it. Declined, expired and withdrawn intro asks
   come back." repeats on Today, Post an ask, Your intros and Ask for an intro. That's the opposite of WhatsApp.
