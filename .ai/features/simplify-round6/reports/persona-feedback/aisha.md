## Closing answers

**The tabs I'd want (in order)**
1. **Today**: live room banner, "Ask for help" box, answers to my asks.
2. **Rooms**: live now, coming up, remind me.
3. **Asks**: post and browse, with intros folded in here as "I want to meet someone".
4. **Inbox**: messages, with intro threads included (the Inbox intro already says "Messages and intro threads").
5. **People**: Directory and Search together, as one tab.

Everything else goes behind my avatar: My profile (card plus Edit), and one Settings page (email, alerts, privacy, data). Perks, Jobs and Library go in a "More" list. Feed: drop it, or merge it into Asks as "Updates".

**What I'd never use**
- Library and the library leaderboard. I'm non-technical.
- The "Muted broadcasts" dropdown.
- The "Our jobs" / Ashby block on My profile.
- "Book a call" setup.
- "Who are you trying to reach?"
- The Feed. It overlaps with "Updates" on Asks.

**Rooms**
Yes, I'd join a fundraising room. "Fundraising in a slow market: open office hours" is the single most useful thing I saw in the whole app. What would get me in: seeing it on Today, a clear "Listen" button, the host's face on stage, sound that starts on its own, and knowing I can stay muted.

I wouldn't start one myself for a while. Notify others when I *join*: no. When I start one: yes, but to chosen people (founders I've talked to, or people who follow fundraising), never "the community". "Alerts the community" is exactly why I'd never tap "I'm available to talk".

**Alerts**
No alerts in the evening after 8pm, on weekends, or while I'm in investor meetings (I'd want a "pause for 2 hours" button). Rooms on fundraising topics are the exception. I'd look for these settings behind my avatar under "Notifications", and also as a bell icon on the Rooms page. Today they're under My profile → "Alerts", a link in a grey line of text ("Email settings · Alerts · Your data") halfway down a 4,750px page.

**Top 5 problems (worst first)**
1. **today / phone tab bar:** the live fundraising room is invisible. There's no Rooms tab, and Today never mentions it; it's only the tiny "Rooms" link under "That's everything this week."
2. **ask:** "Post an ask" opens an intro wizard ("What do you need? A customer / A hire / An investor / An expert… How an intro works"), not a place to type my question.
3. **me:** My profile is overwhelming: 529 words, 52 controls, four Save buttons, plus "Last pulled 20 Nov 01:01 UTC · from Ashby".
4. **rooms_[id] / room-joined:** after joining I can't hear anything or tell I'm in: "Your browser paused the sound. Tap to hear the room", "ON STAGE" empty, "0 LISTENING".
5. **inbox / rooms:** "I'm available to talk… alerts the community" sits on Inbox, inside a private chat and on Rooms. It's scary and easy to tap by accident, and next to "Start a room" its difference is unclear.
