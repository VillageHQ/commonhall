## Closing answers

### Tabs I want
Member tabs (what founders see, phone and desktop):
1. Today (with a "Live now" room strip at the top)
2. Rooms
3. Asks (asks + updates + feed merged; one place to post)
4. People (Directory + Search + Intros: find someone, ask for an intro)
5. Inbox (messages + intro answers waiting for you)

Everything else: Jobs, Perks, Library inside Today as cards and in a "More" menu; My profile, alerts, email, data under
the avatar.

Admin console (my sidebar, in order):
1. Home (this week's pulse + "needs a nudge" list)
2. Engage: Rooms, Announcements, Weekly email (one item, three tabs)
3. Asks & intros (one stuck list, with Nudge)
4. Members (invites, import, calls per member inside)
5. Partner report (with calls and rooms counted)
Then Set up (features, mail, Village, LiveKit, perks, job board, flagged items, audit log) at the bottom.

### Never use
The guided start, the Integrations/Slack page, "Preview as" for announcements beyond a single check, the asks allowance
setting, the cohort/sector/need tables, the 12-week calls table. As an admin: Library leaderboard.

### Rooms
Yes, I'd host and join, and I'd use rooms as my main engagement tool (weekly office hours with a partner or an expert).
What would make founders join: an alert when a room they care about goes live, a Join button in the weekly email and on
Today, and a scheduled room with "Remind me". Notify others: yes, when I start or schedule one. The host should choose:
everyone in the fund for team-run rooms, chosen people (a cohort, a sector) for niche ones. For founder-started rooms,
people who follow that founder. I need attendance counts afterward.

### Alerts
Don't alert me during back-to-back working hours (9 to 12), after 9pm, or on weekends except for rooms I scheduled.
I'd look under my avatar > Alerts, and as admin I want to set a community default quiet window under Set up so founders
start with sane hours. When I announce, I want to see "12 in quiet hours, will get it at 8am".

### Top 5 problems (worst first)
1. Console home (/admin): no engagement pulse. It's a finished checklist, "Four steps to your first partner report",
   with three "Done". Nothing on who's active, what's stuck, or rooms.
2. Asks & digest and Intro log: I can see but can't nudge. "1 can help · 0 answers" and "with connector" with no
   Nudge button, no names of who could help, no days waiting.
3. Member Today and phone tabs: Rooms is invisible. A room is "Live now" with "0 in the room", and the 7 phone tabs
   ("Today, Asks, Feed, Search, Directory, Intros, Inbox") and Today don't mention it.
4. Partner report: counts intros, not engagement. No logins, rooms or calls; "By what the founder needed" is all 0 with
   "Other 13", and its numbers ("24 hours", "5 asks got an offer of help") disagree with Asks & digest ("20 hours", "3 asks
   got an offer of help").
5. Admin Rooms (/admin/rooms): a server page, not a rooms page. "LIVEKIT_URL, LIVEKIT_API_KEY…", "UDP 50000–50100",
   hidden under Set up; nowhere to schedule, announce or see attendance.
