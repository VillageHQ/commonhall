## Closing answers

**The tabs I'd want (in order)**
1. **Today**: only what's waiting for me (intros to approve, asks I can help with), the same as the Thursday email.
2. **Intros**: what I owe and what I'm waiting on. Fold in asks that need me.
3. **People**: Search and Directory merged into one box.
4. **Hiring & perks**: jobs (with "Add a job" and "Find candidates"), perks, and library items for my team.

Everything else lives somewhere smaller. Inbox becomes a badge on the avatar, since I'd reply by email anyway. My profile and settings go under the avatar. Asks & updates and Feed fold into Today. Rooms and Library go under "More". The leaderboard goes away.

**What I'd never use:** Feed, Library leaderboard, "I'm available to talk", "Start a room", "Post an ask" (my team would), "Say hi" on new members, "Is this right?" profile checks after the first week, Block/Report.

**Rooms:** Almost certainly not. I don't drop into live audio between calls. What would make me: a scheduled 30-minute room on a Series B problem (e.g. "First sales hire: what worked"), sent as a calendar invite in the weekly email at a sane hour. The one I saw is "Sat, Nov 21, 6:06 AM", a Saturday at 6 AM. Notify others: no. "Start a room… alerts the community" by default makes me less likely to ever host. If I did host, I'd want to pick the people.

**Alerts:** Push off, always. Email only, once a week, plus one email if an intro for me has waited more than 3 days. No alerts in working hours, evenings or weekends; really, just never on the phone. I'd look under my avatar → settings. But today it's split: "Alerts" says "Email is set apart, under Email", so there are two pages for one question ("how do you reach me?"). Quiet hours are buried under five toggles there. I want one page: "How often may we contact you? Weekly email only / Daily / Instantly".

**Top 5 problems (worst first)**
1. **One intro:** I have to supply the contact myself and choose among three radios where two do the same thing: "Rachel's email, if you say yes" / "I don't know them well enough: Same message to Marcus".
2. **Today:** Nine blocks and 404 words around 3 decisions. The person waiting on me comes after optional asks: "Plus one intro waiting for your yes and one line on your profile."
3. **Ask for an intro:** Six fields, a five-step timeline and a quota rule: "Asks you post and intro asks share it. Declined, expired and withdrawn intro asks come back."
4. **One ask:** Two separate ways to answer on one page ("Write an answer" + "Send" vs "I can help → I know someone / I've done this myself → Offer help").
5. **Search / Directory / profile:** Three places to find a person, and they loop. The profile's "Who Ada can introduce you to" just says "Search", and Directory repeats "fresh as of 8 Nov" on all 20 cards.
