## Closing answers

**Tabs I'd want (in order):** 1) Rooms (live now, coming up, host), 2) Today (asks I can help with and intros waiting,
merged), 3) Asks, 4) Inbox (messages and intros together), 5) People (Directory with Search inside).
Everything else: Feed folds into Asks ("Updates"); Jobs, Perks, Library go under a "More" or the avatar; My profile,
Alerts, Email, Your data under the avatar. Book a call moves into an "Office hours" bit of Rooms plus my profile.

**Never use:** Perks, Jobs (we're 6 people), Library leaderboard, the "Is this right?" profile box on Today, "How you
sign in", "Our jobs", Search on its own tab (it needs my own Village connection; Directory does the job).

**Rooms:** Yes, I'd join, and host weekly. To make me join: the room shown on Today/at the top with real faces and a
count ("Maya + 4 listening"), sound starting on one tap, a labelled "Raise hand". To make me host: schedule with repeat
weekly, a share link, and reminders to people who said they'd come. **Notify others:** yes, when I start or schedule a
room. To whom: I choose each time between people who follow me (default), people I pick, or everyone. Not everyone by
default; "alerts the community" every time would make people mute me.

**Quiet:** No alerts in my focus blocks (weekday mornings, 9 to 12), or after 10pm. Weekends only for rooms I said I'd
join. I'd look for a bell on the Rooms page and in the avatar menu, not under "My profile → Alerts". Quiet hours should
show the times, with weekday choices, and save automatically.

**Top 5 problems (worst first):**
1. Room / after Join: you can't tell you're in, and nobody is visible. "Joining Fundraising in a slow market…",
   "ON STAGE" (empty), "0 LISTENING", "Your browser paused the sound. Tap to hear the room".
2. Rooms: no way to schedule, repeat, share or pick who's told. Only "Start a room" that "alerts the community", and
   "Start now" on my own "Hosted by Sam Lee" card.
3. Today and the phone tab bar: Rooms is hidden. It's not among the 7 tabs, it's a footer link after "That's everything this
   week.", and Today never mentions the live room.
4. Rooms: two host buttons, one caption. "I'm available to talk" / "Start a room", "Opens a room with you as host…
   3 of 3 left today."
5. My profile / Rooms: office hours are split across two pages. "Book a call" is buried mid-page on My profile and
   never linked from Rooms. On top of that, muting is unclear ("Their 'I'm available' broadcasts won't alert you").
