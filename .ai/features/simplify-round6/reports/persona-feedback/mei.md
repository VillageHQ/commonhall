## Closing answers

**Tabs I'd want (in order):** 1) Rooms, 2) Today (with live rooms on top), 3) Search (people and intro paths, with the Directory merged in as its empty state), 4) Intros and Inbox merged as "Messages", 5) Jobs.
Everything else: Perks, Library and Asks go under a "More" or as Today cards. My profile, Alerts, Email and data go under the avatar as "Settings". Feed folds into Today or goes away.

**Never use:** the Library leaderboard, Feed (Asks & updates already covers it), "I'm available to talk" broadcasts, the "Is this right?" profile check on Today, the Directory filters "Cohort or fund" and "Updated: any time".

**Rooms:** yes, at night on my phone, if the topic is technical (data infra, hiring engineers, Series B prep) and I can see who's on stage before I join. A real speaker list, labelled controls and sound that starts on Join would make me stay. A scheduled room needs "Remind me". Notify others: only when I start one, and to chosen people or people who follow me, never "the community". As a listener, never announce that I joined.

**Alerts:** no alerts 9:00–18:00 on weekdays (deep work), none after 23:00, none on weekends except intro asks waiting for my yes. I'd look under the avatar (Settings, then Alerts) or a bell icon, not inside "My profile". Quiet hours must show the actual hours and days.

**Top 5 problems, worst first:**
1. Room (joined): the room looks broken. "0 LISTENING" while I'm in it, an empty "ON STAGE", "Joining…" still showing after I joined, "Your browser paused the sound", and three unlabelled icon buttons.
2. Today and the phone tab bar: a live room is invisible. Rooms isn't one of the 7 tabs, and Today ends with "That's everything this week." while "Fundraising in a slow market" is live.
3. Library: vague and contradictory labels. The filter labelled "Job" means function, "Show" is a button, and the counts differ between "Top this month" (Priya: 5) and her card (7) with no period shown.
4. Alerts: "Quiet hours" is a toggle with no hours, no days and no work-hours option, and it's reached through "My profile".
5. Rooms: three overlapping start actions ("I'm available to talk", "Start a room", "Start now") under one ambiguous caption, "Opens a room with you as host and alerts the community…", plus "0 in the room" on a hosted live room.
