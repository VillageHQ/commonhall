# Omar Haddad: mentor walkthrough (R1, R2, R3)

Who I am: operator-in-residence, mentor in the fund's network. Phone between things, laptop for focus blocks. I'll give
founders a lot of time, but on my terms: office hours at set times, asks in my lane (go-to-market, first sales hires,
ops), and nothing pinging me during deep work. I looked at the phone shots first, then desktop.

## R1: Offer office hours (host a room, share a booking link)

### Today (`member-today`)
- Confusing: "You could help with these 2" then "Plus one intro waiting for your yes and one line on your profile". That's three different kinds of things in one headline sentence. "Each item also works by replying to Thursday's email" is a nice idea, but I don't know which email that is yet.
- Complicated: on the phone I scroll past 2 ask cards, an intro card, a profile check ("Is this right?" with "Right" / "Hide it" / "Fix it"), "Your asks in motion", Perks, Jobs, "New in the library", "New this week". Nine blocks, about 26-30 controls.
- Would skip: "Your asks in motion" (I'm a mentor, not asking), Perks, Jobs, "New in the library", "Asks you post and intro asks share it. Declined, expired and withdrawn intro asks come back." I'd read maybe 120 of the ~410 words.
- Expected: something about rooms. I'm here to host office hours, and Today never mentions Rooms: no live room, no "Coming up", no "host your office hours". On the phone, Rooms isn't in the tab bar (Today, Asks, Feed, Search, Directory, Intros, Inbox). I only found it as a tiny "Rooms" link under "That's everything this week." at the very bottom.
- Fastest path: a "Live now / Your next office hours" strip at the top of Today, and Rooms as a tab.

### Rooms (`member-rooms`)
- Confusing: two buttons, "I'm available to talk" and "Start a room", and one line under both: "Opens a room with you as host and alerts the community, except anyone in quiet hours or who muted you. 3 of 3 left today." Which button does that describe? If "I'm available" also opens a room, how is it different from "Start a room"? Also "3 of 3 left today" of what?
- Complicated: not too many controls (5 on desktop), but the decision between those two buttons stops me.
- Would skip: "Talk live with your community: drop into a room, or say you're free to talk." I'd read "Rooms are never recorded." That one matters to me. About 40 of 96 words.
- Expected: a way to **schedule** recurring office hours ("every Tuesday 4-5pm, 20 minutes each"). "Coming up" shows a scheduled room ("Sat, Nov 21, 6:06 AM · First sales hire: what worked · Hosted by Sam Lee · Start now"), but I see no button to schedule one myself, set it to repeat, or set who it's open to. "Start a room" sounds like right now only. And 6:06 AM is an odd time to show; I'd want my time zone shown.
- Fastest path: one "Host office hours" button → pick now or a time, repeat weekly, who's invited, then done.

### One room / joined (`member-rooms_id`, `member-room-joined`)
- Confusing: "Live" and Maya Ortiz hosting, yet "ON STAGE" is empty and "0 LISTENING", and the list said "0 in the room". Is the host even there? "Joining Fundraising in a slow market: open office hours…" stays on the page after I joined. "Your browser paused the sound. Tap to hear the room" is the first thing I'd have to deal with.
- Complicated: three round icons (a smiley, a speech bubble, a hand) with no labels. I'd guess the hand is "raise hand", but as a host I'd want to know.
- Would skip: little; it's short (48 words). I'd read it all.
- Expected: host controls (bring someone up, mute, end the room), a time box ("15 min each"), and a way to share the room link with a founder who booked. None shown. ("Leave quietly ✌️" is cute and fine.)
- Fastest path: sound plays on the tap that joined; labeled "Raise hand" and "Chat"; host sees "Invite to stage" and "End room".

### My profile: booking link, privacy (`member-me`)
- Confusing: "you" tags beside every field and "from public profiles", plus "“you” marks your own words. Lines “from public profiles” are filled in; your own words always win." I had to read it twice.
- Complicated: this is a 529-word, 52-control page holding my profile card, 11 editable fields, public-source corrections, past roles, Privacy, Book a call, My network, password, and "Our jobs" with "Pull now" / "Hide this company from the board". On the phone, "Book a call" is about two-thirds of the way down, below "Past roles" and "Privacy".
- Would skip: the whole "Filled in from public profiles" block, "My network" paragraphs ("Members who search can see that you know a person…"), "How you sign in", "Our jobs". I'd read maybe 100 words: the card, "Can help with", Privacy, Book a call.
- Expected: Book a call is exactly what I wanted ("Add your booking page and members see “Book a call” on your profile", with "Everyone in Northwind Founders" or "Only people whose intro I accepted"). Good options. But I expected it near the top, or next to Rooms as "my office hours". "Can help with" is what decides which asks reach me, so I expected it to say so. It doesn't.
- Fastest path: a short "Mentor" card at the top of My profile: Can help with, booking link, office-hours schedule, who can reach me. Everything else goes in Settings.

## R2: Answer asks in my area only

### Asks & updates (`member-asks`)
- Confusing: "Ask" vs "Update" toggle; the tabs "Open 6 · Updates 2 · Yours 2 · Answered 2". As a mentor, what's an "Update" to me? "1 member can help · 1 through a warm path" is jargon.
- Complicated: the post box sits on top, though I'm here to answer, not to post. Then six cards and a four-step "How asks work" explainer at the bottom with "(done)" / "(to come)" markers.
- Would skip: the intro sentence, the post box, "4 of 5 asks left this week. We check who in Northwind Founders can help, and tell them.", the whole "How asks work" block. About 150 of ~390 words.
- Expected: a filter for **my area** ("Matches my Can help with" / topics like Sales, Hiring). There is none: only a search box and the Open/Updates/Yours/Answered tabs. The "You could help." boxes are the closest thing, but they're mixed in with asks that aren't mine (the cement plant manager pinned on top).
- Fastest path: a "For you" tab first (asks that match my expertise or my network), with everything else under "All".

"Paths come only from members who agreed to share their network. Nobody else is named." That's reassuring, but it's a third explanation on one card.

- Expected: "Offer a call" using my booking link, which is the way I actually help. Nothing on this page connects to "Book a call" or to rooms ("bring it to my office hours").

## R3: Control when the app interrupts me

### Alerts (`member-me_alerts`)
- Confusing: "This device: Alerts are blocked for this site in your browser's settings. Allow them there, then come back." That's on top, and it isn't something I can fix on this page. "“I'm available” broadcasts" vs "Voice rooms opening": two kinds of room pings I can't tell apart.
- Complicated: the switches themselves are fine (master + 5 kinds). But it's reached by My profile → "Alerts" link in small grey text after "Save privacy" ("Email settings · Alerts · Your data"). Email alerts live on a separate page ("Email is set apart, under Email").
- Would skip: "Off means no alerts on any device." and the muted list's 19 names. About 60 of ~150 words.
- Expected: real **quiet hours**: "Quiet hours: Nothing alerts you in these hours" is a single toggle, and no hours, days or time zone are shown on phone or desktop. I want "weekdays 9-12 focus, evenings after 7, weekends off". I also expected "Voice rooms opening" to let me choose **whose** rooms (people I follow, my mentees) rather than all-or-nothing. And "Muted broadcasts" is an opt-out dropdown of every member, backwards for me: I'd rather opt in to a few.
- Fastest path: Settings → Alerts: one "Focus schedule" (pick hours and days), room alerts set to "Only from people I follow", and email and push on the same page.

### Inbox (`member-inbox`, `member-inbox_id`)
- Confusing: an "I'm available to talk" button, with "Opens a room with you as host and alerts the community… 3 of 3 left today", sits on top of my private messages. In my inbox? One tap and the whole community gets pinged, which is the opposite of protecting my focus.
- Complicated: inside a thread, the same broadcast button again above the conversation; plus "Report", "Delete", "Block Priya".
- Would skip: "Messages and intro threads. Reply here or by email; both land in the same thread. Northwind Founders' admins can't read messages in the portal." Fine once, but it's repeated in the thread. About 30 of ~77 words.
- Expected: intro asks and messages in one place. "Messages and intro threads" says they are, but the intro waiting for me is under "Intros" (badge "1"), not here.
- Fastest path: Inbox = messages + intro asks waiting for my yes, no broadcast button.

## Closing answers

**The tabs I'd want (in order)**
1. Rooms (live now, my office hours, schedule one)
2. Asks: "For you" first (asks in my area), then All
3. Inbox: messages and intro asks together (absorb Intros)
4. People: Directory and Search merged
5. Me: profile, booking link, alerts, email, data, all in one Settings area

Everything else: Feed folds into Asks (updates are just posts). Jobs, Perks and Library go into a "More" under Me or a
"Portfolio" page. "New this week" stays a line on the Rooms/home screen. "Our jobs" goes into company settings, not my profile.

**What I'd never use:** Perks, Library and its leaderboard, Jobs / "Our jobs" ("Pull now", "Hide this company from the
board"), Feed, "Your asks in motion", the "Is this right?" public-profile check, "Post an ask" (I answer, I don't ask),
password ("Add a password").

**Rooms:** Yes, this is the main reason I'd open the app: scheduled weekly office hours, 30-60 min, founders drop in,
"never recorded". What would make me: scheduling with a repeat, my time zone shown, host controls (bring up / mute /
end), and founders who booked a call getting the room link automatically. Notify others: yes when I *start my
scheduled* office hours, to people who follow me or booked/asked me before (chosen people), not "the community". And
I'd want a once-a-day digest of rooms opening rather than a ping per room. I would not use "I'm available to talk" as a
community-wide ping.

**Alerts / quiet:** No alerts in my focus blocks (weekday mornings 9-12), after 7pm, or on weekends, except a
founder I've accepted an intro with messaging me directly. Rooms from people I follow only, the rest batched. I'd look
under Me → Settings → Alerts (today it's My profile → the small "Alerts" link). The quiet hours there need actual hours
and days, not one toggle.

**Top 5 problems (worst first)**
1. Alerts: quiet hours is one switch with no hours, days or weekends: "Quiet hours: Nothing alerts you in these hours", but which hours? Room alerts are all-or-nothing ("Voice rooms opening").
2. Rooms: no way to schedule recurring office hours, and two unclear buttons share one caption: "I'm available to talk" / "Start a room", "Opens a room with you as host and alerts the community… 3 of 3 left today."
3. Today / phone tab bar: Rooms is absent from the 7 tabs and from Today; it's only a footer link under "That's everything this week."
4. Asks: no "my area" filter; tabs are "Open 6 · Updates 2 · Yours 2 · Answered 2", and an ask nobody can help with is "Pinned" on top.
5. My profile: "Book a call" is buried two-thirds down a 529-word page between "Past roles" and "My network", and nothing links it to asks or rooms.
