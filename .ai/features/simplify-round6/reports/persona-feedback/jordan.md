## Closing answers

### The tabs I'd want (desktop sidebar)
1. **Today** (what needs me, plus my asks)
2. **Asks** (post in one line, browse; answers and "I can help" here)
3. **People** (Directory + Search merged; filter by "can help with")
4. **Perks & jobs** (one "Save and hire" page: perks with Claim buttons and peer "who uses this", jobs with "Post a job", "Find candidates" on mine, library hiring/ops items)
5. **Inbox** (messages and intro threads together; Intros folds in here as "Waiting for your answer")

Everything else: Rooms as a small link or a banner when something is live. Library under Perks & jobs or a link from
Today. Feed merged into Asks (it's the "Update" toggle anyway). My profile, alerts and email under the avatar. Leaderboard gone.

### What I'd never use
Library leaderboard. Feed. The "Update" toggle on Asks. The "I'm available to talk" broadcast. The "Fund / Cohort"
and "Updated" filters in Directory. Search as its own tab: it "needs your own Village connection" and I won't connect one.

### Rooms
Maybe, rarely. I'd join a scheduled, topic-specific room like "First sales hire: what worked" if it's on my calendar
with a reminder, not "drop in". A live "Fundraising in a slow market" room with "0 in the room" won't pull me in. What
would: a named host who's done the thing, a fixed 30 minutes, and a calendar invite.
Notify others: **no** for "I'm available". Yes, to **chosen people** (or people who followed the topic), when I
schedule a room. Never "the community".

### Alerts
Quiet during focus blocks (9-12), evenings after 7pm, and all weekend. Only intro asks waiting for me and direct messages
should ever break through, and even those batched once a day would be fine. I'd look under My profile → Alerts (where it
is). But today "Quiet hours" is one toggle with no visible days or times, and "Voice rooms opening" and
"'I'm available' broadcasts" are on by default. They should start off.

### Top 5 problems, worst first
1. **/ask**: "Post an ask" on Today opens the intro wizard ("What do you need? A customer / A hire / An investor / An expert", sidebar on "Intros"). There's no vendor or recommendation option, and /asks already has a one-line box.
2. **/perks**: no way to claim anything (0 controls). Nothing shows which vendors peers actually use. "BuiltFirst may ask you to sign in with your BuiltFirst account."
3. **/jobs**: you can't post a job from the jobs page: "Hiring? Add your company's jobs on My profile." "Find candidates" appears unexplained on some rows.
4. **/inbox**: a community-wide broadcast button in my private inbox: "I'm available to talk: Opens a room with you as host and alerts the community."
5. **/library**: jargon and too many filters. "Skill / Agent / Prompt / Tool connection / Automation / Playbook" plus a "Job" dropdown with a "Show" button, plus a leaderboard I'd never open.
