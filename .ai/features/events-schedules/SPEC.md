# Spec: Events and schedules: fund events with RSVP, calendar feeds and reminders

**Added:** 2026-11-19 (round 5, wave 1)

## What & why
Funds run demo days, dinners, AMAs and office hours, but Commonhall has no events. Founders keep a separate calendar invite chain for each.

## Rough scope
An events module: create an event (time, place or link, host, audience), RSVP, a calendar file per event and one private calendar feed members subscribe to, reminders, a slot on Today and in the weekly email. Office hours stay on booking links.

## Open questions
- Paid or capacity-limited events.
