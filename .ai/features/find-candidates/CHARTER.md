# Charter: Commonhall round 4 — live tour, grouped features, 4 new features

Approved by the owner on 18 Nov 2026 (decision page "Commonhall: live tour + 4 new features").

## Direction (the owner's words, condensed)
- The landing page's product demo must walk the major journeys: search the network with faces and real-looking people,
  ask one person for an intro or post the need as a request to the community, browse the directory, book a call with a
  mentor or expert, find candidates for a portfolio job, the fund team sends an announcement, the admin sees it counted.
- It must be the app's actual UI (an interactive, auto-playing copy like village.ai's landing demo), not screenshots in
  the old look, and sit centred in the window.
- The feature boxes lose "Built / Partly" and sort into groups (meet and connect, raise, grow, hear from the fund).
- New app features: announcements (mail-merge to the community), mentors / experts / advisors / entrepreneurs in
  residence, booking links with "Book a call" on profiles and the directory, calls counted in the admin, find candidates.

## The owner's answers
- The landing page lists everything, planned features too, with no labels.
- "Book a call": each person's own booking link; the admin counts each "Book a call" click (no confirmed-booking sync).
- Goal matching (a founder states a goal, Commonhall finds people; maybe via Telegram): not now, noted for later.

## Small calls (Qareen's, on the page)
- Each person sets who sees their booking link: everyone in the community, or only people whose intro they accepted.
- Announcements email only members who have not unsubscribed; the team sends a test to itself first.
- Finding candidates needs the founder's own Village connection, like network search.
- The tour plays on a timer on phones and stays still for visitors who turn off motion.
