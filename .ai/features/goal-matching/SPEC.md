# Spec: match a founder's goal to people automatically

**Added:** 2026-11-18

## What & why
The owner's idea: a founder states a goal and Commonhall finds the right people in the network for it on its own,
perhaps through a Telegram chat. On 18 Nov 2026 the owner chose "not now": community requests (asks) already reach
likely helpers.

## Rough scope
Options weighed: inside the app (a goal, then 3 matching people a week), or a Telegram bot that suggests people.

## Open questions
- Is a separate goal different enough from an ask to be worth a new channel?
