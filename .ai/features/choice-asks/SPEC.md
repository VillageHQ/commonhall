# Spec: asks and intros, decided in one tap

What this part does now. The round's whole plan, its evidence and the council's decisions are in
[less-choice/SPEC.md](../less-choice/SPEC.md) (Part 1); the approved scope is [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md).

- **Matching reads the ask and whom it names together.** The ask's own word matches keep their places among the 5
  word helpers; the folded target only fills places left, so naming someone never finds fewer helpers.
- **The Asks box is one box and Post.** Once something is typed: "Post a win instead" (posts the same text as an
  update), the folded "Name who you're trying to reach", and the week's asks-left line. Enter posts. The box grows to
  show the whole sentence on a phone.
- **An intro ask** shows the asker's note first, then "Introduce" and "No thanks". Introduce shows the private link
  with "Copy link" first and "Copy with a message" quiet beside it; "Email it to <name>" is a folded second step,
  shown only when the community sends email. No thanks is one kind of no; the asker never hears why. The emailed
  ask and its public answer page keep their three answers (the email's words are Commonhall's, checked word for word).
- **The drafted intro note** names the person asked for (name, role, company) and the asker from their own card;
  never an earlier need from the profile. On the profile ask it follows the person's boxes until the asker edits it.
- **"I can help"** commits on the first tap from Today. Its public line is fixed ("I can help with this." or "I know
  <person> and can introduce you."); a note written on the ask's page is private to the asker and its writer.
- **"Not for me"** (Today, and Asks › For you) hides the ask from Today, For you and the weekly email; Open and search
  still show it.

Checks: the five in [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md), proven in [reports/VERIFY.md](reports/VERIFY.md).
