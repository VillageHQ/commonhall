# Spec: AI vendor trust exchange: security reviews of AI vendors done once, reused by all

**Added:** 2026-11-19 (round 5, wave 3)

## What & why
Security is the top blocker to AI adoption (51% in a 2026 survey); every company redoes the same vendor review.

## Rough scope
A shared page per AI vendor: security answers, the review one company did, notes from others, with confidentiality settings.

## Open questions
- Legal wording for shared reviews.
