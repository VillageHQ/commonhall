# Spec: Mentors portal: mentor home, requests, call limits, and "was the call useful?"

**Added:** 2026-11-19 (round 5, wave 1)

## What & why
Mentors-calls added roles, the Experts and mentors view, booking links and call counts. Mentors still have no home of their own, cannot cap their calls, and the fund never learns whether a call happened or helped.

## Rough scope
Role on the single invite form; a mentor home (requests, upcoming calls, what founders asked); a monthly call limit per mentor; a follow-up after each Book a call ("Did the call happen? Was it useful?") that feeds the partner report. Later: requests the mentor accepts first, matching, office hours.

## Open questions
- Who sees a mentor's call limit and answers.
