## Other things I saw (not failures)
1. **Chips 14 px under the search box, not 12.** I measured the gap from the search field's bottom to the chips' top at 14 px (390 and 1280). The outline puts them 2 px lower than the page (check 11), still within 8 px.
2. **"Keep editing" drops the focus.** After it, `document.activeElement` is `<body>`, not the text box. On a phone the keyboard would close and need another tap. Returning the focus to `#p-body` would fix it.
3. **The composer isn't dimmed behind "Discard this ask?".** Both sheets are z 71 and both scrims z 70, so the second scrim sits under the first sheet. It works (tapping the composer area closes only the question), but it reads flatter than a dimmed stack. This comes from Part 1's `Sheet` (sheet-over-sheet).
4. **The web card has no Cancel.** With text in it, Escape does nothing. The only ways back to the prompt row are to empty the box and press Escape, or to leave the page. That matches the part file (Escape folds an empty box), but a member who changes their mind has no button.
5. **Tap targets under 44 px** (Accessibility: "every tap target is at least 44×44"):
   - "more" under a cut ask is 22 px tall (tapping the body opens the ask too);
   - the meta line's "Mark answered", "N answers", "You could help" and For you's "Not for me" are 32 px;
   - "Fix it" (a `<summary>`) is 20 px;
   - the install card's ✕ "Hide this" is 16×24 (the install-card file is unchanged; Part 4 owns only its buttons, which are 36 px: Install and Alert settings).
6. **At 300 px a short name can be cut.** When "Pinned 5 days ago" shares the name line, "Amara Diallo" becomes "Amara Dia…". The search placeholder "Search every ask and its answers" is clipped at 300 (the placeholder is unchanged from before this part).
7. **Switching to a win moves the box up about 6 px.** The allowance line goes, so the author block gets shorter (judged from the pictures; not measured).
8. **The sheet runs into the demo banner in the app.** The sheet's top at `--sat` (47) overlaps the 52 px demo banner, which shows dimmed above it (demo only).
9. **At 0 asks left, Post stays enabled.** The server's refusal then appears in the sheet. The weekly cap shows the clock note: "You've used this week's 5 asks. The next one comes back 27 Nov.". The open-asks limit shows a plain red line: "You already have 5 open asks. Close one, then post this.". A hiring request from Jobs › Find candidates opens the sheet prefilled, with "A hiring request for your job doesn't use one of your weekly asks.", and posted at 0 left (`?m=posted`). A hand-made `?ask=` doesn't prefill: the link uses `?draft=`.
10. **`/asks?compose=1` and `/asks#compose` open the right composer.** They open the sheet at 390 and the card at 1280, with the focus in the box. The server HTML holds only the prompt row, so the wrong composer never flashes.
