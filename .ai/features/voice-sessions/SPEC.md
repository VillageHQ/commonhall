# Spec: Voice sessions: scheduled live audio rooms, recorded and summarized

**Added:** 2026-11-19 (round 5, wave 2)

## What & why
Open social audio faded (Clubhouse pivoted, LinkedIn Audio Events closed Dec 2024), but small scheduled sessions (AMAs with partners, AI show-and-tell, office hours) work, and their know-how is lost after the call.

## Rough scope
LiveKit rooms (Apache, self-host or LiveKit Cloud at about $0.03 per participant-hour): host, speakers, listeners, raise hand, linked to an event; with the host's consent a recording, transcript and AI summary posted to the feed and offered as a draft library item.

## Open questions
- Recording consent wording; cloud or self-host by default.
