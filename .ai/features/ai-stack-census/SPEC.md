# Spec: AI stack census: which AI tools portfolio companies tried, kept or dropped

**Added:** 2026-11-19 (round 5, wave 2)

## What & why
Adoption is measured, outcomes are not: AI SDR tools churn 50-70% a year and most company AI pilots stall. Founders cannot see what worked for peers.

## Rough scope
A short survey on the profile (tools tried, kept, dropped, why), shown as "kept rate" by tool and stage only when 5 or more companies report. Feeds the partner report.

## Open questions
- Whether answers are named or always anonymous.
