# Spec: member settings in one place

The full spec, its checks and the council's decisions: [less-choice SPEC, Part 3](../less-choice/SPEC.md). The approved
scope and the small calls: [CHARTER.md](CHARTER.md). How it works: [NOTES.md](NOTES.md).

## What it does now
- One Settings list, the same in the avatar menu and at the foot of My profile: My card, Privacy, Alerts, Email,
  Booking link, Your network, Our jobs, Your data, Password (Your network and Our jobs while their feature is on).
- Intro asks are switched on Privacy only; My network keeps its own sharing consent and points there.
- "Nobody" hides a member from People and from the asks' word matching, and says so in one line.
- Let through starts off for members who joined after round 7; earlier members kept theirs on.
- One "Edit card" opens every card field with one Save.
- "Who can message me": Anyone, or Only people I've been introduced to.
