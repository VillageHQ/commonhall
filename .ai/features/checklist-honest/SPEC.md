# The console's launch checklist: what counts as done

On the console Home of a new community (`src/shell/console-launch.ts`):
- **Your fund's look** is done when the branding is saved and a logo is set (uploaded or a Logo URL).
- **Invite your first founders** is done once an invite reached someone: emailed, or used to join. Its words count the invites that were emailed: all
  emailed → "N invites sent"; none → "N added, no email sent yet" with a link to Sign-in links; some → "N added, M emailed".
- **Mail and the network** links to /admin/mail and /admin/village.
