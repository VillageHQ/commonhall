The Inbox reads like the messengers people use: one row shape with 48 px faces, the time on the right, "You: " on your own last line and a dot or count for unread; a thread is its own screen with the person's face and name at the top, messages as flat rows (a tap shows the time and Delete or Report), and a reply bar pinned to the bottom; on the web the list and the open thread sit side by side.
