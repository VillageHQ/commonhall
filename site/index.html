What's inside

Meet, raise, grow, hear from the fund, and share what works in AI.

Meet and connect

  • Network searchWho your members know, with the member who can introduce you.
  • IntrosNobody is introduced without saying yes.
  • PeopleWho is here, and how they help.
  • MessagesOne-to-one, between members.
  • Mentors and expertsMentors, experts, advisors and entrepreneurs in residence, marked on their profiles.
  • Book a callEach person’s own booking link, on their profile and in People.
  • Voice roomsLive rooms like Clubhouse: a stage, an audience, raised hands and reactions. Never recorded.
  • Start a roomOne tap starts a room and alerts everyone; each member chooses what reaches them.

Raise

  • Investor introsPost what you are raising for; members who know the investors can introduce you.
  • Paths to investorsSee which member knows a fund or an angel, and ask them.

Grow

  • Customer introsReach buyers through the members who know them.
  • Job boardJobs from 12 hiring tools, read every hour.
  • Find candidatesSearch the portfolio’s networks for the hire you need.
  • PerksYour perks marketplace, inside the portal.

Hear from the fund

  • AnnouncementsOne email from the fund to the whole community.
  • FeedAsk, Win and Offer posts.
  • Chat postsAsks go out to Slack, Discord, WhatsApp and Telegram.
  • Admin reportsEvery intro, ask and outcome, counted, and a partner report to send.
  • Phone alertsIntros, answers, messages and live rooms on your phone, with quiet hours and mutes.
  • The phone appInstall the portal on your home screen from the browser; the App Store and Google Play app follows.

AI across the portfolio

  • AI libraryFounders share the AI skills, agents, prompts, tool connections and playbooks that work for them.
  • Upvotes and a leaderboardUpvote what helped; the leaderboard ranks the people and companies who share most.
  • Sorted by kind and jobFind a sales prompt or a hiring agent in two taps.
  • Safe to shareEvery upload is checked for keys and passwords, and the author picks who sees it.

The real app

Walk through the app, as members and admins use it.

Type who you need, then search their networks in one tap. See the faces in reach and the member who can introduce you.

Ask one member for the intro. They say yes first, then the person you want to meet.

Or ask everyone. The members most likely to help hear about it, and it goes in the weekly email.

Every founder in one place. Filter by stage, sector, city and cohort.

Mentors, experts and advisors in one view. Book a call on their own calendar, in one click.

Hiring? Find candidates for a job in the network around you, or ask the community for them.

Founders share the AI skills, agents and prompts that work for them. Upvote what helped; Top shows what others use most.

Live voice rooms, like Clubhouse. One tap starts a room and alerts everyone; each member decides what reaches them. Never recorded.

Alerts on your phone and computer. Pause them for an hour, or keep working hours quiet.

The team writes once. Every member gets their own email, and it is pinned in the feed.

The fund's home: this week's active founders, asks, intros and room joins, and what needs you.

The app's own screens, playing on made-up people and companies.
